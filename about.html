What is LifeQuest?

LifeQuest is a gamified, modular web application designed to help users develop important life skills in an engaging and interactive way. By combining practical tools with a progress-based system, LifeQuest encourages users to build positive habits, manage responsibilities, and work toward meaningful personal goals.

Our Purpose

The goal of LifeQuest is to make personal development more approachable and motivating. Many people struggle to stay consistent with habits, budgeting, and productivity. LifeQuest provides simple tools that help users stay organized, track progress, and remain motivated over time.

Features of LifeQuest

LifeQuest includes several modules that focus on different areas of personal growth. These include tools for managing finances, building daily habits, improving productivity, and unlocking achievements as progress is made. Each feature works together to support users on their personal growth journey.
